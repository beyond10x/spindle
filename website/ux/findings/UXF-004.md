# UXF-004: "how do I serve it over HTTP?" has no way in

The answer is the section "Serving `ingress.network` over HTTP" of the Capabilities page. The
sidebar, the navbar and the landing page carry neither "HTTP" nor "serve"; the section's title
shows only in that page's own table of contents. A reader who does not already know that HTTP
is a capability (`ingress.network`) reads Getting started and the overview to their ends and
stops.

Cost S: a sidebar link under Concepts to the section, labelled with its heading.
