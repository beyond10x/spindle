# UXF-012: "2 of 6" is followed by "1 of 6"

A reader paging with Next goes from Getting started ("2 of 6") to The specification ("Concepts
· 1 of 6") and through six concept pages, all "of 6", then to Rally, CLI and Status, which carry
no position at all. Two counts share one wording, and the outer one stops after its second page,
so the label cannot tell a reader how much is left.

Sessions: all page checks of the 8 docs pages with a numbered kicker (32 of 48).

One line: name the set in every kicker ("Start · 2 of 2", "Concepts · 1 of 6") or drop the top-level count.

Cost S: the kicker text in the shared theme's doc header.

## Closed in R-02

C4: Overview and Getting started carry the front-matter kicker "Start", which the shared header shows instead of "1 of 6" and "2 of 6"; the concept pages keep "Concepts · n of 6". The asset ran red on `.out/R-02-build` (1 failed: 2 pages) and green after.
