# UXF-003: links in prose differ from the text only by colour

In paragraphs the theme draws links without an underline, in a green with too little contrast
against the body text for a reader who does not see colour well (WCAG 1.4.1). The shot shows
"the rally example" in a sentence: only its hue marks it.

Cost S: underline links inside the article's running text in `website/src/css/custom.css`;
navigation, cards and headings keep their look.
