# UXF-008: the sidebar leaves with the article when the footer comes in

The sidebar is sticky inside its column; the column ends where the article ends, so once the
footer scrolls in, the sidebar's top 385 px leave the viewport (only CLI and Status stay). The
footer repeats every page link, so nothing is lost, but the reader's place in the sidebar is.

Cost L: a layout change (the sidebar column, or a shorter footer). Not in R-00; a candidate for a
later round's decision.
