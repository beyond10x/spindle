# ESS hardening round, 2026-11-23: Spindle's own specification

The specification is `ess/` (`spindle.deployment`, `spindle.environment`, `spindle.placement`,
`spindle.observe`, `spindle.console`). The implementation held to it is `HostTarget`
(`crates/spindle-conformance`), Spindle's kernel running the compiled model. Base: `main` at
`bbc50b00`, ESS 0.98.0. Before the round `task check:conformance` executed 79 of 79 scenarios, 0
skipped, all passed. The question was whether that green result catches anything.

The round followed the ESS `hardening` skill. Every technique that ran was first shown to fail on a
defect planted for it. Findings are numbered F1 to F20 and listed in the `findings` block at the
end.

### 1. Mutation audit

`ess verify conform mutate --path ess --emit` wrote 79 mutants (18 stillborn, 17 with synthesis
refusals the baseline lacks) and 142 sites. `b10x-spindle conform` refuses a suite of another
specification (`Unprepared::Foreign`), so it cannot be the runner of an emitted mutant suite; a
throwaway test ran each suite against `HostTarget` on the unchanged specification instead, and
`--collect` scored the reports.

On the original specification `HostTarget` killed 37, 3 survived and 21 were unwitnessed (no
synthesized scenario reaches the mutated site). The survivors: `guard-boundary` on
`DecideAdmission/not-decided/equality-0`, `refused/0` and `refused/0-outward`. Of the 21
unwitnessed, 17 sit on `DecideAdmission` (`missing-provided`, `not-provided`, `not-decided`,
`refused`, `admitted/requires`), the outcomes synthesis cannot arrange (F2 to F4); 2 on
`LaunchInstance/not-admitted`, which the authored `launch-not-admitted` covers; and 2 are
`DeclareEnvironment/declared/os` and `/arch`, which no view published (F6).

`--target interpreted --scenarios ess` runs the authored scenarios against the interpreter of
each mutant, so an authored scenario kills there. Before the round: 44 killed, 13 survived, 4
unwitnessed. After F1 to F3: 54, 6, 1. After F4 and F6: 60 killed, 1 survived, 0 unwitnessed. The
one survivor is `refused/0-outward` (F8). The 16 mutants that moved are listed per finding in
"What changed".

### 6. Guard analysis

Read from the compiled IR (`ess specify compile --format json`). Guarded commands:
`DeclareEnvironment` (`key-twice`, `no-implementation`), `OfferBundle` (`unknown-environment`),
`DecideAdmission` (`bound-twice`, `not-decided`, `not-provided`, `missing-provided`, `admitted`,
`refused`), `PlanInstance` (`unknown-deployment`), `LaunchInstance` (`not-admitted`). No entity
declares an invariant, so no guard can admit an invariant break.

- Dead: none. Every guarded outcome is reached by the walk (technique 2).
- Gaps: none. Each guarded command ends in an unguarded branch (`declared`, `offered`,
  `unconfirmed`, `planned`, `launched`).
- Overlaps: `key-twice` with `no-implementation`, and `bound-twice` with `not-decided`, answered
  by precedence. Both pairs give the same error and no payload. ESS lists the second swap as
  identical, and the first is the same kind.
- `admitted` (`missing.count == 0`) and `refused` (`missing.count >= 1`) split the input exactly;
  the `refused/0-outward` mutant (`>= 0`) cannot be told apart, because `admitted` answers 0 first
  (F8, `equivalent`).
- Weak input type: `Resources` took any `Integer`, while every launcher holds `u32`/`u64`; a
  negative ceiling or request was declared and planned (F5).

Planted: in a copy, `refused` guarded by `missing.count >= 2` leaves `missing.count == 1` to
`unconfirmed`. ESS validated the copy, and the by-hand partition found the gap at 1. A copy whose
`admitted` reads `missing.count <= 1` takes a decision with one missing capability before
`refused`. ESS validated that copy too; only the authored scenario `admission-refused` refused it
(`ESS-AUTHOR-041`, its declared outcome is not the one selected). Neither the compiler nor the
synthesized suite checks that guards of one command partition the input.

### 2, 4 and 5. Walk, determinism, metamorphic relation

`crates/spindle-conformance/tests/spindle-conformance/explore.rs` holds them as tests. 150 seeds
of 40 steps each; the first 16 steps of a seed only create or decide rows (declare an
environment or a target, offer, admit, plan, place), so later commands meet rows in every state.
The value pools are small (2 environments, 2 deployments, 2 targets, 3 instances, 3
capabilities, 4 provision keys), so steps collide on the same rows. Half of the admission
decisions are consistent, so the branches after `not-decided` are reached. No resource is
negative (F13). Before a command with `external:` outcomes, one of them is forced half the time. Each step is sent to `HostTarget` and to `ess_conformance::interpret::Interpreted` on the
same IR, which compares the outcome, the error and its fields, the events and their payloads, and
the rows of all four views. A disagreement is shrunk by dropping steps. Every outcome `ess/`
declares must be reached.

Result: all 34 declared outcomes reached, no disagreement, 0 divergences between the two runs of
a sequence. Planted (`Defect::FirstRowOnly`): the walk failed at seed 0, shrunk to 2 steps (two
rows of one view). The first walk, of any command from the first step, reached
`PlaceInstance/environment-mismatch` and `above-ceiling` in no seed: 7 of 379 forced
`PlaceInstance` sends met a `Planned` instance. The setup steps fix that.

The walk found three differences, none a defect of `HostTarget`:

- A negative resource (F13): ESS's interpreter answered `PlanInstance` with `unknown-deployment`,
  where the kernel refuses the input first as no value of `Resources`. ESS 0.98.0's interpreter
  checks a struct input's invariants after its input-guarded refusals. The walk sends no negative
  resource; `placement::a_resource_below_zero_is_refused` pins the kernel's answer.
- A `creates:` for an identity a row already holds (F15): no outcome is declared for it. The
  kernel answers no outcome and changes nothing; the interpreter refuses the request ("not what
  the model declares"). The walk counts both as "no declared outcome", and the views must agree.
- An absent `Optional` field in a view row (F20): the interpreter publishes it as `null`, the
  kernel leaves it out. The walk compares rows without `null` values.

Determinism: each sequence is run twice on fresh targets, and the answers must be equal. The
source scan found clock reads in the kernel (`civil_now`), but `ess/` declares no `now` guard, no
`Timestamp` input and no generated value, so none reaches an outcome. The mint is never asked,
since no identity is generated. No `HashMap` is iterated on a command path. Planted
(`Defect::Unstable`): every second target the process opens answers no declared outcome, as a
clock or a hash order would make it; the walk failed at seed 0, step 0.

Metamorphic: the design says that on an `Offered`, `Refused` or `Stopped` deployment
`StopDeployment` "is accepted and changes nothing" (§ 3a), and `ReportExit`'s `already-ended`
says the same of an instance. For each walk, the first step answered `already-ended` is removed,
and every other step must answer the same. 148 of 150 walks held such a step, and in each the
relation held. Planted (`Defect::TraceOfNoOp`): the step after a no-op answered no declared
outcome, and the relation failed at seed 0 (a `ReportExit` answered `already-ended`, step 28).

## Not changed

- F7, a note on procedure and not a finding: `b10x-spindle conform` cannot run an emitted mutant
  suite, because it refuses a suite of another specification. Mutation audits need a runner of
  their own until it can. No story was filed.
- F8: `refused/0-outward` is equivalent by precedence. ESS does not detect it.
- F12: nothing compares `ess/` with the last release in the gate:
  `story:spindle-spec-diff-gate`.
- F13: ESS 0.98.0's interpreter checks a struct input's invariants after the command's
  input-guarded refusals, where the kernel refuses the input first. Limit of `ess`.
- F14: ESS accepts an error field that no outcome gives a source. Its interpreter then leaves the
  field out, where a served application fills it from the row, as Spindle's kernel does (F1).
  Limit of `ess`.
- F15: a `creates:` for an identity a row already holds has no declared outcome: row G2 of
  `story:spindle-spec-design-review-2026-11-23`.
- F16: `DeclareTarget` refuses no environment nobody declared, while `OfferBundle` does: row G1 of
  the same story.
- F17: neither the compiler nor the synthesized suite checks that a command's guards partition its
  input; both planted copies validated (technique 6). Limit of `ess`.
- F18: 22 design-review findings are decisions for the design's owner:
  `story:spindle-spec-design-review-2026-11-23`.
- F19: about forty line citations in `console.yaml` and `observe.yaml` are unchecked, two of them
  stale: `story:spindle-spec-cites-source-by-symbol`.
- F20: the interpreter publishes an absent `Optional` as `null`, the kernel leaves it out. Both
  mean the same; the walk compares rows without `null`.
